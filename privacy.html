Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

This policy covers the automation apps Oberne Creek Consulting runs for its clients using Google APIs.

What the apps access

  • Gmail, read only: the apps read business reports that a client has chosen to forward to the Oberne Creek Consulting mailbox. They do not send, change or delete email.
  • Google Drive, limited to files the app creates: the apps write the documents they produce into a folder they created, which is shared with the client.

How the data is used

Data is used only to produce the documents the client has engaged us to automate, such as a weekly invoice. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and retention

Client data stays in Google Gmail and Drive. It is not kept in source code repositories. When an engagement ends, access is revoked and the client's data is returned or deleted at their direction.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
